What is this file ▼

The central member roster for WIT Canada. Tracks everyone on the team — their info, onboarding progress, Google Group memberships, and tool access.

Working sheets
WIT_Members
Main roster — internal Canada team. One row per member. All onboarding tracking lives here.
WIT_External
Global WIT members with access to specific Canadian groups. No onboarding flow — group access tracking only.
Auto-generated report sheets
Report_Groups
Snapshot of every Google Group and its members. Rebuilt on demand — never edit manually.
Report_Email_Requests
Members with a pending WIT email creation request (status: requested, sent, or created).
Report_Photos&Bios
Members missing a profile photo or bio in the shared Drive folders, grouped by region.
Past-year tabs (e.g. 2025) are archived — historical data only.
Who does what ▼
L
Team Lead
Adds new member to the sheet, ticks Google Groups, sets Email Status to requested.
O
Ops Lead
Assigns group access; sets Email Status to created when the account is ready — the onboarding checklist email sends automatically. Member Status flips to onboarding / active on its own.
M
Member
Activates email, follows the Welcome Guide checklist independently.
Report_Groups sheet ▼

A read-only snapshot of every Google Group — who's in it and their role. Pulls data from both WIT_Members and WIT_External.

Members from WIT_External appear in italic grey to distinguish them from internal members.

Rows are collapsible — use the group indicators on the left to fold and unfold each list.

Refresh via ⚙️ Tools → Refresh groups snapshot. The sheet is deleted and recreated each time — never edit it manually.
Some column sections are hidden by default. To expand them, look for the [+] button.

Member Management ▼
Contract status
Tracks the volunteer agreement. Values: not sent → sent → signed. The Lead updates this manually. See the Statuses tab for details.
Email Status
Drives the WIT email creation flow. Values: — → requested → sent → created → not activated → active. The Lead sets requested — the Ops Lead updates the rest. See the Statuses tab for what each value triggers automatically.
Member Status
Overall standing of the member. Values: — → onboarding → active. Updated automatically — do not edit manually. See the Statuses tab for conditions.
Add to groups
Tracks Google Group access. Values: — → requested → added. The Lead sets requested — Ops updates to added once access is assigned. See the Statuses tab.

Contract Status ▼
not sent Default. No contract action taken yet.
sent Contract has been sent. Waiting for signature.
signed Contract signed → a prompt offers to open the signed-contracts Drive folder so you can upload it.
Email Status ▼

Controls the WIT email creation flow. The Lead sets requested — the Ops Lead updates the rest.

— Default when the row is created. No action taken yet.
requested Lead sets this → an activation heads-up email goes to the member's personal inbox, Ops is notified, and the member is queued for the Monday batch report.
sent The weekly batch report was sent to the Ops Lead. Waiting for WIT account creation.
created WIT account created → the onboarding checklist email auto-sends to the member's WIT inbox.
not activated Email created but activation pending. Follow-up required.
active Member activated their WIT email. No email is sent at this stage.
Add to Groups ▼

The Lead sets requested. Ops updates the rest.

— Default. No action taken yet.
requested Lead ticked the required groups and set this status. Ops will assign access.
added Member added to all required Google Groups. Drive and Chat access granted automatically.
Member Status ▼
— Default. Member added but onboarding not yet started.
onboarding Set automatically while onboarding is in progress — contract signed, email pipeline underway, groups pending.
active Set automatically once contract is signed, email is active, and groups are added.
How group access works ▼

Google Groups are how we control access. Being in a group gives the member email, Shared Drive access, and Google Chat membership — all from one place.

Groups are also used for email distribution. When an email is sent to a group address, all members of that group receive the message.

Canada Team
Every internal member. Gives access to the WIT Canada General Shared Drive.
Leadership
Leads and heads. Gives access to the Leadership Shared Drive.
All other groups
Topic or region-based. Access scoped to that team's resources.
Always use Groups to grant access — never add someone to Drive or Chat directly.

External members ▼

Global WIT members with access to specific Canadian groups — usually via NA Awards or partnerships. Tracked in WIT_External.

No onboarding flow. Manage their group access the same way — tick the columns, then run the sync.

In the Report_Groups sheet, external members appear in italic grey to distinguish them from internal members.

To access these actions, click ⚙️ Tools in the top menu bar (after "Help"). Each item opens a submenu: Member Onboarding, Documentation, and Actions.

Actions ▼
Refresh Email Requests
Rebuilds the Report_Email_Requests sheet — all members with a pending WIT email request (status: requested, sent, or created).
Refresh Photos & Bios
Scans the Photos and Bio folders in Drive, updates the Photo and Bio columns for all members, then opens Report_Photos&Bios with the results by region.
Refresh Groups
Rebuilds Report_Groups — a clean list of every group and its members from both WIT_Members and WIT_External.
All three reports open automatically after refreshing.

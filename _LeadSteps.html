Add the member ▼
1
Add a new row in WIT_Members
First name · Last name · Role · Location · Personal email
2
Send the contract
Send manually, then set Contract Status = sent.
3
Member signs → mark signed & upload
Set Contract Status = signed → a prompt pops up offering to open the signed-contracts Drive folder. Click Upload now and drop the signed copy in. auto
Request email & access ▼
4
Request WIT email
Set Email Status = requested → confirm the dialog.

Automatically: an activation heads-up email (telling the member their WIT account is being set up) is sent to their personal inbox, the Ops Lead is notified, and the member is queued for the Monday batch email to Admin. auto
5
Select groups & request access
Check all required Google Groups columns based on the member's role and region. Then set Add to Groups = requested.

This is where your work ends — Operations takes over from here.
What happens automatically: Every Monday at 7 AM, a batch email is sent to Admin with all pending requests. Status flips requested → sent automatically after the email sends.
Ops takes over here — coordinates account creation with Admin and assigns group access. The steps below happen once the account exists.
Account created → onboarding ▼
6
Account created → mark it
Once Admin creates the WIT account, set Email Status = created → confirm the dialog. the onboarding checklist email (account ready — complete your checklist) auto-sends to the member's WIT email. auto
7
Member activates → mark active
After the member activates their account, set Email Status = active. No email is sent at this stage.
Member Status is automatic: it becomes onboarding while in progress, then active once the contract is signed, email is active, and groups are added. auto
Definition of done ▼

A member is fully onboarded when all of these are true:

✓ Contract signed & uploaded
✓ WIT email active
✓ Added to Google Groups
✓ Drive & Google Chat access granted
✓ Onboarding checklist complete
Member Status = active — set automatically once all of the above are true.
